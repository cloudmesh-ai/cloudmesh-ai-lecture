    - Understand the institutional foundations, collaborative history, and continuous evolution of the course materials.
    - Recognize how modern tooling, including Large Language Models, supports the development of the course ecosystem.
    - Embrace the role of a living-document collaborator by actively contributing improvements, bug fixes, and content updates.

# Acknowledgements

We gratefully acknowledge the resources, tools, and collaborative effort that make this course possible.

* *Institutional Framework:* Portions of this material is is developed to support classes at Loyola University Chicago (LUC), building upon foundations and selected topics originally developed and taught in classes at Indiana University, as well as contributions conducted for research at University of Virginia. 

* *Evolution & Student Contributions:* A significant portion of the core content has evolved across previous iterations of our classes and research activities benefiting greatly from the direct contributions, feedback, and hard work of past students.

* *Modernization & LLM Integration:* Since 2026, we have actively integrated advanced Large Language Models (LLMs) into our workflow to improve instructional content, add modern sections, and update and modernize our codebases.

* *Living Course Materials:* The lecture notes and course materials provided here are considered drafts. Students in the current class are expected to actively engage with these resources, suggesting improvements, fixing bugs, and adding new material as part of their learning journey.

* *Technology & Tools:* Special thanks to the open-source communities behind Python, Flutter, Slurm, and the various local and remote orchestration frameworks utilized in our hands-on labs.
