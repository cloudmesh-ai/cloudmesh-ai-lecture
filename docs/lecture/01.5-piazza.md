# Piazza Discussion Forum

!!! info "Learning Outcomes"
    - Establish access to the official course communication and Q&A platform.
    - Apply collaboration best practices by prioritizing public discussions for general course questions.
    - Recognize appropriate guidelines for utilizing private instructor messaging and engaging with peer support.

We use **Piazza** as our online communication platform for class-related discussions, announcements, and Q&A. Using Piazza helps us keep all course communications organized and ensures that everyone benefits from shared questions and answers.

!!! tip "Quick Start"
    Join the class page immediately at: [https://piazza.com/class/mt5rkdsycb31c3/](https://piazza.com/class/mt5rkdsycb31c3/)

## Guidelines for Use

* **Check Before Posting:** Before creating a new thread, please check if your question has already been asked and answered by your instructors or classmates.
* **Ask Publicly First:** If you have a question about homework assignments, lecture material, or logistics, please post it publicly on Piazza. This allows your peers to learn from the answer and helps instructors avoid answering the same question multiple times.
* **Active Participation:** Helping your peers answer questions is strongly encouraged. Teaching a concept to someone else is one of the best ways to solidify your own understanding.
* **Community Standards:** We are committed to providing a welcoming and inclusive environment for all students. Harassment of any kind will not be tolerated.

!!! warning "Private Posts & Security"
    **Private posts are rarely needed for class-related information.** Use them only for sensitive questions concerning personal matters, grades, or specific accommodations. For highly sensitive issues, you may also email gvonlaszewski@luc.edu.

    **Security Warning:** Never post secure information, such as private keys or passwords, on Piazza, regardless of who asks for them.

## How to Ask a Great Question

To get the fastest and most accurate help, please include the following in your posts:

1.  **Clear Title:** Instead of "Help!" or "Question," use "Issue with Exercise 2: Python Subprocess error."
2.  **Context:** Explain what you are trying to achieve and what you expected to happen.
3.  **The Problem:** Provide the exact error message or a description of the unexpected behavior.
4.  **Your Efforts:** Briefly mention what you have already tried to fix the problem.
5.  **Code Snippets:** Use the code block feature to share relevant parts of your code. Avoid uploading large files unless requested.

## Response Expectations

We strive to maintain an active and supportive forum. You can generally expect:

*   **Instructor Response Times:** Public inquiries are typically addressed within 24 to 48 hours. Please note that response times may be slower over the weekend (from Saturday morning through Monday evening) due to other professional commitments.

*   **Peer Support:** Many questions are answered quickly by your fellow classmates.

By following these guidelines, we can create a high-quality knowledge base that serves the entire class throughout the semester.

## Turning Answers into Contributions

If you provide a detailed, high-quality answer to a peer's question on Piazza, consider converting that answer into a permanent contribution to the lecture notes. While Piazza is great for immediate help, the lecture notes serve as a lasting resource for current and future students.

To turn your Piazza answer into a contribution:

1.  **Refine the Content**: Expand your answer, add clear examples, and ensure it is formatted well in Markdown.
2.  **Contribute via GitHub**: Follow the process outlined in the [Contributing to the Lecture Notes](lecture/01.2-contribute-lecture-notes.md) guide to submit your improvement as a Pull Request.

This not only helps the entire class but also builds your public portfolio of technical communication skills.
