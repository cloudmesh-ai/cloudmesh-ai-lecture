# ACCESS CI Account Setup

ACCESS (Advanced Cyberinfrastructure Coordination Ecosystem: Services) is the gateway for researchers and educators to obtain allocations for high-performance computing and cloud resources, including Jetstream2.

## 1. For Students: Getting Access Credentials

If you are a student joining a course or a research project that already has an allocation, you do not need to apply for your own allocation. Instead, you need to create an ACCESS account so the PI (Professor/Lead Researcher) can add you to their project.

### Steps to get your credentials

1. **Create an Account:** Go to the [ACCESS Portal](https://my.access-ci.org/) and register for a new account.
2. **Complete Your Profile:** Fill out the required user information.
3. **Notify Your PI:** Once your account is created, send your **ACCESS username** or the **email address** you used for registration to your Professor or Project Lead.
4. **Wait for Invitation:** Your PI will add you to the allocation. You will receive an email notification once you have been granted access.
5. **Login:** Once notified, you can log in directly to [Jetstream2](https://jetstream2.cloud.iu.edu/) using these credentials.

---

## 2. User Onboarding

Once a user has been added to an allocation:
1. They should log in to the [Jetstream2 Login page](https://jetstream2.cloud.iu.edu/) using their ACCESS credentials.
2. They will then have access to the project resources and can start launching instances.

## APPENDIX (For PIs/Faculty)

!!! warning "Not to be done by students."
    This sections in the Appendix is for PIs (Instructors, Professors, or research Leads) who are PI eligible. Applying for an allocation is not allowed to be done by students.

### A1. Applying for an Allocation (For PIs/Faculty)

!!! warning  "Not to be done by students."

To use Jetstream2, the PI must first obtain an allocation through the ACCESS portal.

**Steps to apply:**

1. **Register:** Create an account on the [ACCESS Portal](https://my.access-ci.org/).
2. **Request Allocation:** Submit a request for a Jetstream2 allocation. You will need to provide a brief justification (e.g., for a specific course or research project) and specify the amount of resources needed.
3. **Approval:** Wait for the ACCESS review committee to approve your request.
4. **Activation:** Once approved, your account will be activated, and you can log in to the Jetstream2 interfaces.

### A2. Managing Your Allocation (For PIs/Owners)

!!! warning "Not to be done by students."

**Steps to add users:**

1. **Log in to the ACCESS Portal:** Go to [my.access-ci.org](https://my.access-ci.org/).
2. **Navigate to "My Projects":** Locate the project/allocation you own.
3. **Manage Members:**
   - Go to the **"Members"** or **"Manage Users"** section.
   - Click **"Add Member"** or **"Invite User"**.
4. **Invite Users:**
   - Enter the email address or ACCESS username of the person you want to add.
   - **Note:** The user must have already created their own ACCESS account before they can be added to a project.
5. **Assign Roles:** Assign a role (e.g., "Member") and save the changes.
